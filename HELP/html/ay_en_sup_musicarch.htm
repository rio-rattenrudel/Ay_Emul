Music archives

The Ay_Emul author has prepared an archive of music in the format of various ZX Spectrum editors: ZX Spectrum Tracker Music (Tr_Songs). The archive contains several tens of thousands files in the format of popular ZX Spectrum music editors and thousands playlists. You can find it on the official site of Ay_Emul in the "Archives of music" section.

In the same section, the archives collected or put in order by the Ay_Emul author are published: Amstrad CPC AY Music Collection, Turbo Sound Music Collection and others.

There are other resources, here are just some:

  • http://kay27.narod.ru/ay.html – the author's archive of the musician Hacker KAY on his home page.
  • In the Web-archive: http://sorry.vse.cz/dimension/rdos/rdosplay/ – archives of music in AYM and FXM format on the RDOSPLAY site.
  • https://worldofspectrum.org/projectay/ – a huge archive of AY files collected by bcass and like-minded people.
  • http://pacidemo.planet-d.net/aldn/ – YM Archive by aldn (archive of music from Atari ST in YM format).
  • http://sndh.atari.org/ – SNDH music archive maintained by Phil Graham and other enthusiasts from the Atari ST platform.
  • http://xagon.webz.cz/software/software.htm – the author's archive of the musician X-agon on his home page.
  • http://ay-riders.speccy.cz/ – creativity of the AY-musicians group "AY Riders".
  • https://ymrockerz.creamhq.de/downloads.php – creativity of the YM-musicians group "YM Rockers".
  • http://sky-killers.narod.ru/#music – the author's archive of the musician mR T.A.D. on his home page.

Unfortunately, number of resources on which in previous years it was possible to download music archives for AY-3-8910 and YM2149 are getting decreased over time. On the official site of Ay_Emul in the "Archives of music" section and on its FTP server, you can find some of the missing.
