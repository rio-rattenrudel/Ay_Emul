Authors

Two men from Khabarovsk made Ay_Emul:

Sergey Bulba (svbulba@gmail.com) as author.

Ivan Reshetnikov as main graphics maker.


Icons was provided by Sergey Bulba, Ivan Reshetnikov, David Willis, bcass, X-agon, Graham Goring, Exocet and Roman Morozov.

Noise emulation algorithm of AY-3-8910 and YM2149F is written with Hacker KAY (Hacker KAY read a complete array of bits from the noise generator of a real chips, with the help of which Sergey Bulba recreated the schematic diagram of the generator).

AY-3-8910 and YM2149F output level measured and designed as tables by Hacker KAY.

Different formats players are written by Sergey Bulba and based on original players and (or) descriptions.

For MC68000 emulation used Starscream 680x0 emulation library by Neill Corlett (corlett@elwha.nrrc.ncsu.edu), modified by Stéphane Dallongeville and Carsten Elton Sørensen.

For some YM2-files playing used sample datas from digidrum.cpp of Open Source ST-Sound Library by Arnaud Carré.

Ay_Emul can work with BASS/BASSENC libraries by Ian Luck and with SNDH music duration database from sc68 project by Benjamin Gerard (if corresponding files are present in Ay_Emul folder).
