Playing order

Ay_Emul plays items from playlist either from first to last item, or from last to first one, or randomly, or only one current item. Each of four playing order modes can be selected by serial pressing of corresponding button in playlist window.

After playing last item, playing starts from begin of selected playing order if Loop playlist's button is in pushed state, otherwise playing is stopped.

Random playing order is defined by generating new list in background, therefore no item will be played twice, and also you can back to any previous played item. The random play order is redefined on loading, adding, changing or sorting playlist, re-selecting the mode, or on double-clicking or Enter key pressing on any playlist item.
