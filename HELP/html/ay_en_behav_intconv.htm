How to abort the conversion

During the conversion, the main window of Ay_Emul becomes active, and the Progress slider turns into a progress indicator of the conversion process.

In most cases, it is enough to wait for the conversion to finish (the slider will move to the rightmost position).

But if necessary, the process can be interrupted at any time by pressing the Esc key or by left-clicking on any part of the main window.

Even an incomplete converted file is a correct module of the appropriate type, which can be listened to in Ay_Emul or in another suitable player.
