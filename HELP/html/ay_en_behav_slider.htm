Playback position changing

Ay_Emul allows you to restart playing from any point of the tune during its listening. To change playback position, click by left mouse button on Progress bar or move the slider to desired position on main window.

Also, you can press cursor keys "Left" and 'Right" for reposition 5 seconds backward or forward correspondingly.

You can call "Jump to time" dialog by pressing J hot key. In appeared dialog type desired time in seconds.
